Boarding Deposits And Cancellations

Know the terms before confirming a stay.

These terms apply to bookings of three nights or more and to every booking made for a listed peak period.

Deposits

When a deposit is required

  • The standard boarding deposit is one night’s stay—currently $63 plus HST for one pet.
  • Every stay of three nights or longer requires a deposit.
  • Every booking during a listed peak period requires a deposit, regardless of length.
  • Stays longer than two weeks may require a deposit equal to 25% of the total booking. Contact the Resort for details.

Peak periods

Dates with non-refundable deposits

  • February Break.
  • March Break.
  • Easter Weekend.
  • Summer Season: June 1–August 31.
  • Holiday Season: December 15–January 3.

Changes To A Stay

Refunds, credits, no-shows, and early departures

  • For a non-peak booking, cancel at least seven days before the stay to receive a full deposit refund or an account credit for future use.
  • Deposits are non-refundable for no-shows and for non-peak cancellations received fewer than seven days before the stay.
  • Peak-period deposits are non-refundable if the booking is cancelled.
  • Leaving boarding early may result in an early-departure charge equal to 50% of the unused remainder of the stay.

Deposit and cancellation terms may change without notice. The Resort will confirm the applicable deposit and terms when your reservation is made.

Refunds, Returns And Appointment Cancellations

How refunds are calculated and which purchases qualify.

Keep your receipt, original packaging, and UPC when requesting an eligible product return.

Daycare Packages

Unused package value

A daycare package refund starts with the amount paid, then subtracts every used day at the full daily rate. A further $50 fee applies to a one-dog 10-day package; a $100 fee applies to every other package. No daycare-package refund is issued after one year of inactivity.

Group Training

Class deposits and course refunds

Class deposits are non-refundable. If you are dissatisfied, the course price—less the deposit—may be refunded at the end of the first class. No refund is issued after attending two or more classes.

Training programs are not currently running; this policy is retained for eligible training purchases covered by the original terms.

Food

30-day food returns

Eligible food purchases may be returned for a full refund within 30 days. Bring back the original bag or frozen box, including its UPC, with at least 50% of the contents remaining.

Retail Products

10-day product returns

Unopened and undamaged collars, toys, harnesses, and other retail products may be returned for a full refund within 10 days. Products damaged through unsupervised use are not eligible for a refund.

Grooming Appointments

Cancellations and pickup

  • Provide at least 24 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule a grooming appointment.
  • A $10 late-cancellation fee may apply when sufficient notice is not provided.
  • Prepayment or a deposit may be required for the next appointment.
  • Pick up your dog within one hour after the appointment is finished. Regular daycare or boarding fees may apply after that time.

Daycare Late Pickup Fees

After the daycare closing time.

Once daycare closes, staff need to prepare meals and settle overnight guests. Late-pickup fees help protect that care time, and all fees collected are donated to the Nova Scotia SPCA.

If you expect to be delayed, contact the Resort as soon as possible. If the team cannot reach you or your emergency contacts after 30 minutes, your dog will be boarded safely.

Late Fee Schedule

Fees after closing

  • Up to 15 minutes late: $10.
  • 15–30 minutes late: $15.
  • More than 30 minutes late: regular boarding rates plus daycare fees apply if neither you nor your emergency contacts can be reached.

Pet Store Discounts

For eligible retail purchases.

  • Military and first responders: 10% off pet retail purchases with identification.
  • Seniors age 65+: 15% off pet retail purchases on the last Thursday of each month.

Please note

Retail only.

These discounts do not apply to daycare, grooming, boarding, or training services.
