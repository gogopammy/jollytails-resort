Guest Requirements

A safe start for every social dog.

Daycare and boarding guests follow the same core eligibility requirements. These steps help the Resort team build comfortable playgroups and prepare for each visit.

Request An AssessmentExplore Daycare

Core Requirements

Before daycare or boarding.

Age And Social Fit

Eligibility.

  • At least 16 weeks old.
  • Spayed or neutered at six months of age or older.
  • Comfortable participating in supervised group daycare.
  • Successful Jollytails Resort daycare assessment.

Vaccinations And Health

Current records required.

  • Rabies.
  • DA2PP/DHPP, covering distemper, adenovirus/hepatitis, parainfluenza, and parvovirus.
  • Leptospirosis.
  • Annual Bordetella.
  • Free of fleas, ticks, and signs of contagious illness.

At Daycare

Safe play gear.

  • Quick-release collar.
  • No hanging tags, trackers, or AirTags while in daycare.
  • No harnesses, choke collars, prong collars, or electronic collars in the play areas.

Assessment And Attendance

Getting to know your dog.

New dogs begin with an interview and careful introduction to staff dogs and an appropriate playgroup. A successful assessment continues with a half-day visit at the regular partial-day rate.

To keep the Resort familiar, daycare and boarding guests should attend at least once every three months. A dog away longer may need another assessment.

Other Locations

A Resort assessment is still required.

Client profiles, assessments, and packages are not shared between Jollytails locations. A dog attending another location needs a separate Resort profile and assessment.

Next step

Does your dog meet the requirements?
