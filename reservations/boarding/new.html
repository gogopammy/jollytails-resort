New Boarding Guest

Plan your dog’s first Resort stay.

New boarding guests need a Resort daycare assessment before their stay. Begin at least two weeks ahead so there is time for the introduction and a daycare visit.

Before You Begin

Things to know.

  • Complete the assessment form. It will take approximately 10 minutes.
  • Include your preferred arrival and departure dates.
  • Include your dog’s feeding routine, sleeping preferences, and medication details.
  • Provide current vaccination records and emergency contact information.
  • Let us know your availability for the required Resort daycare assessment.
  • After you submit the form, a member of our team will be in touch within a day or two.

What happens next?

An assessment is your dog’s first visit. You’ll stay for about 30 minutes while we introduce your dog safely and comfortably to the playgroup. If the introduction goes well, your dog will stay for a half-day visit. This gradual start helps your dog adjust happily and successfully.
