Program Information

Two stages of early dog development.

Review both program outlines in one place.

Puppy Classes

A confident start for life together.

Puppy Classes are designed to help families build practical foundations during an important early stage. Training is not currently available at Jollytails Resort.

01

Communication

Build clear, consistent ways to guide and reward your puppy.

02

Everyday Skills

Practice useful foundations for home life, handling, and new environments.

03

Confidence

Support calm, positive experiences as your puppy learns about the world.

Surviving Adolescence

Support for the teenage-dog stage.

Surviving Adolescence is designed around the changing focus, energy, and behaviour that can arrive as puppies grow. Training is not currently available at Jollytails Resort.

Focus

Reconnect Attention

Practice useful ways to regain focus around everyday distractions.

Calm

Build Self-Control

Support calmer choices through consistent, practical routines.

Life

Strengthen Daily Skills

Keep important foundations working as confidence and independence grow.
