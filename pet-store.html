Choosing A Food

Start with the pet in front of you.

Quality food matters, but there is no single format that suits every dog or cat. The Resort carries high-meat, limited-ingredient, grain-inclusive, and grain-free foods so customers can compare recipes rather than being pushed toward one approach.

When reading a label, look at the full recipe and how it fits your pet’s life stage, preferences, sensitivities, and routine—not just the front of the bag. The team has researched the products it stocks and can help explain the differences.

What To Compare

A practical label checklist.

  • Named animal proteins rather than vague ingredient descriptions.
  • A protein, fat, and carbohydrate balance that suits your pet.
  • Limited-ingredient or single-protein recipes when useful.
  • Clearly identified fats and oils.
  • No unnecessary artificial flavours, colours, or preservatives.
  • Grain-inclusive and grain-free choices based on individual needs.
