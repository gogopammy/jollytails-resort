The Jollytails Resort difference

Where dogs come to play, stay, and feel at home.

Jollytails Resort is locally owned and operated in Bayers Lake, bringing daycare, overnight boarding, grooming, and pet supplies together under one roof. Our team is here to make every visit comfortable for your dog and simpler for you.
