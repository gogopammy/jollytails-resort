A Resort Stay

A familiar rhythm from daytime to bedtime.

Boarding is designed for social dogs who are comfortable in group daycare. Days include indoor and outdoor play, rest, feeding, and individual attention. At night, each guest settles into a private sleeping space.

Daily email report cards help you stay connected while your dog is with us.

Pack For A Comfortable Stay

What to bring.

  • Your dog’s regular food, with enough for the full stay.
  • A familiar bed or blanket.
  • Favourite toys, bones, or safe chews.
  • Clearly labelled medication and instructions, after confirming the team can administer it.

Please leave dishes and electronic toys at home. Refrigerated and frozen food can be stored at the Resort.

Timing

Check in before 1pm.

Boarding guests check in before 1pm so they have time to settle into the day. Checkout is by 2pm; daycare fees apply after 2pm.

Before You Book

Who boarding is for.

All boarding guests must meet Resort daycare requirements.

  • Social dogs who can participate comfortably in group daycare.
  • Dogs with a successful Jollytails Resort daycare assessment.
  • Dogs who have attended Resort daycare or boarding within the past three months.
  • New guests who complete their assessment at least two weeks before the planned stay.

Clients from another Jollytails location still need a Resort profile and assessment because client records and packages are not shared between locations.

Boarding FAQs

Everything from sleeping arrangements to booking, pickup, and daily updates.

Where does my dog sleep?

Jollytails Resort has several different sleeping arrangements for overnight guests including kennels and crates. Staff select sleeping arrangements based on size (for example, a larger dog has priority over a smaller dog for a kennel) and on where your dog is most comfortable. We very much appreciate insight about your dog’s sleeping habits; for example, if your dog always sleeps in a crate at home then it makes sense for us to continue that at Jollytails Resort.

Does my dog have to be an existing Jollytails client to board?

It is not necessary to be an existing Jollytails client. Before boarding at the resort, your pooch must complete our daycare social assessment and should attend daycare at least once every three months before a boarding stay at Jollytails Resort. If it has been over three months since we have seen your dog, we will require a reassessment prior to boarding. This helps your pup remain familiar with our facility, staff, and routine between boarding stays.

How far in advance should I book my dog’s visit?

Reservations are required for all boarding stays. We recommend making your reservations as early as possible to guarantee space for your pup.

New clients who have not previously stayed at Jollytails Resort are encouraged to make reservations at least two weeks in advance. This will allow time for a social assessment visit for your pup, and a day or two of daycare if needed, prior to their stay.

Unfortunately, we are unable to accommodate last minute and same days reservations for dogs who have not had an assessment visit at Jollytails. Due to staffing requirements for these visits, additional fees may apply for last-minute assessment appointments.

If my dog is currently a client at another Jollytails location, do I need to provide a new assessment form?

Yes. Jollytails Resort does not have access to client information at other Jollytails locations. A new assessment form enables us to create your account at Jollytails Resort and provides us with important, up to date information about your dog. Next steps are determined on a case-by-case basis.

My dog doesn't get along with other dogs - can you still board them?

Although we offer separate kennels for sleeping, we focus primarily on social activity for dogs. Guests spend their daytime in group play and interaction throughout the day. If your dog does not get along with other dogs, an in-home boarding arrangement may be the best option for you.

Does my dog need to be spayed/neutered to attend Jollytails Resort? What vaccinations are required?

To stay and play at Jollytails, dogs must be over 16 weeks of age and spayed or neutered (if at least 6 months old). You must provide proof of core vaccines (DHLPP - Distemper, Hepatitis, Leptospirosis, Parvovirus and Parainfluenza as well as Rabies) and a yearly vaccine for Bordetella (canine cough). Your dog must be flea and tick free and must pass our daycare assessment.

What are your drop-off/pickup time deadlines?

Overnight boarding guests must be dropped off by 1:00 PM on the day their boarding begins so they have lots of time to play with all of their friends before bedtime. If you are unable to drop your pet off by 1:00 PM, please let us know.

We are open for reserved boarding pickup and 1:00 PM drop-off 365 days a year. Holidays may change pickup and 1:00 PM drop-off procedures, so please ask us for the applicable holiday arrangements.

The overnight boarding fee includes daycare for the duration of your pup’s stay, including their departure day, until 2:00 PM. Pickups after 2:00 PM will incur additional daycare fees, as per our price list.

What do I need to bring?

If your pet has a favourite bed or blanket, you are welcome to bring it along. You can also bring their favourite toys, bones or chews. You should also ensure that your dog has more than enough of their usual food for their stay at the Resort, as many dogs burn more calories during daycare than they would at home, and can require increased amounts of food to maintain their healthy weight. If your pup eats a type of food that we sell at the Resort, you can purchase a bag here for their stay. The Resort has a dedicated food preparation area that is equipped with a fridge and freezer for raw or other perishable food.

What can I leave at home?

We ask that you leave any toys with batteries or electronics at home, as well as dishes. Our kitchen is fully stocked with stainless steel food and water bowls, as well as slow-feed dishes.

What is the latest I can pick up my dog?

You can come in anytime during our regular business hours when our front store is staffed. Please note that we are open for boarding pickup and 1:00 PM drop-off 365 days a year. Holidays may change pickup and 1:00 PM drop-off procedures, so please ask us for the applicable holiday arrangements.

If you are staffed 24/7, why am I limited to regular business hours to drop off or pick up my dog?

Boarding drop-off is by 1:00 PM. Outside of business hours, Jollytails Resort staff are busy attending to the dogs who are spending the night at the resort. In order to give guests the most attention, resort staff are not able to accommodate pickup outside of the regular hours. If there is a special circumstance or emergency, please let us know and we will do our best to make arrangements.

What happens if my dog gets sick?

Our first priority is the health and safety of your dog. If at any time our staff feel that your dog requires emergency veterinary care, we will attempt to contact you. If we are unable to reach you or your emergency contact, or if your dog appears to be in immediate distress, we will bring your dog to a veterinary hospital for treatment.

My dog has special feeding/medication requirements. Can you administer my dog's medications?

Under most circumstances, yes. Please don’t hesitate to ask us about any special requirements for your pet.

How will I know how my pet is doing?

We send daily report cards by email, letting you know how your dog is doing. Our clients love these updates! The report card includes information on behaviour, play, and other activity. Please note that on Sundays and holidays, you may not receive a report card. You are welcome to contact us to check in on your pup at any time. Our staff will get back to you as quickly as possible.

How do I book?

New and existing clients can click “Make a Reservation” above and follow the prompts.

Deposits and Cancellations

The deposit and cancellation policy applies to bookings of 3 nights or greater, as well as to all bookings during these peak periods:

  • February Break
  • March Break
  • Easter Weekend
  • Summer Season (June 1 – August 31)
  • Holiday Season (December 15 – January 3)

The boarding deposit is equivalent to one night’s stay (currently $63 plus HST for one pet). Cancellations received seven or more days in advance are eligible for a full refund or credit for future use. Deposits are non-refundable for no-shows and for cancellations later than seven days in advance.

Deposits during the periods above are non-refundable in the event of cancellation. Bookings for periods of greater than two weeks may require a deposit of 25% of the total. Please contact us for details.

Early boarding departures may be subject to an early departure charge equal to 50% of the remainder of the stay.

Our deposit and cancellation policy is subject to change without notice. We will confirm any deposit requirements at the time that you make your reservation.
