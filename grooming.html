The Jollytails Resort Difference

Comfort comes first.

We do not rush appointments. Our groomers take the time needed to support a safe, comfortable grooming experience and a result you are happy with, slowing down when a dog needs it.

Our electric-rise grooming tables have large, non-slip tops that safely accommodate dogs up to 250 pounds. The lift adjusts from 15 to 40 inches, so dogs do not have to jump up or down from a height that could cause injury.

We use high-quality shampoos, conditioners, wipes, and spritzes made with human-grade ingredients and pure essential oils. They are natural, non-toxic, paraben-free, phthalate-free, and phosphate-free, without artificial colours or fragrances.

Complete Grooming Prices

Every service and starting price in one place.

All prices are plus 14% HST. Full-groom prices are estimates; final pricing depends on coat condition, the amount of de-matting required, behaviour, and the requested cut.

What a full groom includes

A bath, shampoo, conditioner, blow dry, nail clipping, ear cleaning, and hair clipped in the desired cut.

Groomers assess coat condition before beginning. Dogs with excessive matting require a signed Matted Dog Waiver.

Appointment Policies

A smoother visit for everyone.

  • Dogs must be picked up within one hour after their appointment is finished. Boarding or daycare fees may apply after that time.
  • Please provide at least 24 hours’ notice to cancel a grooming appointment.
  • A $10.00 late-cancellation penalty may apply. Prepayment or a deposit may also be required for the next appointment.
  • Coat condition, excessive de-matting, behaviour, and the requested cut can affect the final price. A Matted Dog Waiver is required when excessive matting is found.

Good to know

Anal gland expression is not offered.

In Nova Scotia, anal gland expression is considered a medical procedure and can only legally be performed at a veterinary hospital.
