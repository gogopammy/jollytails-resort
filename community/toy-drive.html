A new toy can make a shelter pet’s day.

The Jollytails Dog and Cat Toy Drive is collecting new, unused toys for Nova Scotia shelter animals. Donations begin making their way to shelters on December 22.

Find The ResortAsk About The Toy Drive

How To Help

One simple donation. A lot more play.

Drop off a new, unused dog or cat toy at Jollytails Resort or another participating Jollytails location.

  1. 1

    Choose A New Toy

    Select a new, unused toy appropriate for a dog or cat.

  2. 2

    Drop It Off

    Bring it to Jollytails Resort at 15 McQuade Lake Crescent during regular store hours.

  3. 3

    Help A Shelter Pet

    Deliveries begin December 22 to Nova Scotia shelters, including the Nova Scotia SPCA.

Donation Details

What the Toy Drive accepts.

  • New, unused dog toys.
  • New, unused cat toys.
  • Donations delivered to a participating Jollytails location before distribution begins.

Questions? Email info@jollytailsresort.ca.

Distribution

Deliveries begin December 22.

The 2026 drive supports Nova Scotia animal shelters, including the Nova Scotia SPCA.

Next step

Ready to donate?

Stop by the Resort during regular pet-store hours with a new dog or cat toy.
