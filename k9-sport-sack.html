What Is K9 Sport Sack?

A safe, forward-facing way to adventure together.

K9 Sport Sack carriers let dogs ride upright on their person’s back. They are designed to bring you closer to your best friend and open up trips or places that might otherwise be out of reach.

The carrier is not a replacement for exercise. It gives your dog a break during a hike, bike ride, commute, shopping trip, or another shared outing.

  • Bring a smaller dog along on everyday travels, including the bus, bike rides, and shopping.
  • Help a senior dog or a dog with mobility concerns spend more time by your side.
  • Give a tired dog a break on a longer hike or adventure.
  • Let a little dog travel comfortably—and in style.

How To Select A Size

Start with your dog’s back length.

Bring your dog to Jollytails Resort for help selecting a carrier, or begin with K9 Sport Sack’s online guide. Measure from the base of the neck, where the collar sits, to the base of the tail. Weight can help narrow the model, but the back-length measurement is essential for choosing the size.

A secure fit supports comfort and safety for both dog and wearer. Product ranges and sizing can change, so use the current manufacturer guide or confirm the fit with the Resort team before purchasing.
